About KPR Smart Food Court

Smart Dining for a Modern Campus

The KPRIET AI-Powered Pre-Order App is designed to solve daily campus dining bottlenecks. By allowing students and staff to order their meals ahead of time, we eliminate long queues during peak break hours.

Our integrated AI recommendation engine analyzes order history, dietary preferences, and peak timing to suggest healthy and delicious options tailored specifically for you.

Why Use Our Platform?

Zero Wait Time

Pre-order your meals before break time begins and grab your fresh food as soon as you arrive.

AI Recommendations

Receive smart food suggestions customized to your taste, dietary preferences, and nutritional goals.

Seamless Payments

Pay quickly using student wallet integration, UPI, or online payment gateways securely.
