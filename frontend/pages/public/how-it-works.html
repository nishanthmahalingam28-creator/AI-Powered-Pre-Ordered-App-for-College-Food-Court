How The AI Food Court Works

STEP 01

Login / Sign Up

Register with your official Institute Email ID & Student Roll Number to access personalized services.

STEP 02

Get AI Suggestions

Browse menu items or let our smart AI recommend meals based on your food history and preferences.

Select your pickup time, add items to your cart, and complete seamless payment online or via wallet.

Skip Line & Pick Up

Head to the designated pre-order pickup counter when your notification fires and enjoy your hot meal!
