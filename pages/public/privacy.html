Public policy

Privacy Framework

This page describes how the food court application is intended to handle information. It is an informational frontend policy and should be reviewed and adapted by the institution before production use.

1. Account information

When an account is created, the service may receive details such as name, institute email, mobile number and customer type. These details help identify the customer and support account-related communication.

2. Order information

Orders may include selected items, shop, order reference, pickup status, timestamps and pickup verification details. This information is used to prepare, fulfil and support orders.

3. Payment-related information

Payment providers may process payment details when a payment method is connected. The frontend does not store card numbers or payment credentials. Actual retention and processing depend on the selected payment provider and institutional systems.

4. Security

Access controls, secure transport, limited staff access and appropriate credential handling should be used to protect account and order information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and local storage

The demo frontend may use browser local storage for temporary cart behavior. Production services may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, preferences and essential functionality. Users can manage browser storage through their browser settings.

6. Data sharing

Information may be shared with the relevant food vendor, payment provider or authorized campus staff when needed to complete an order or provide support. Information should not be shared for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

7. User choices and rights

Subject to applicable institutional policy and law, users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their account information, and may ask questions about how their information is used. Requests can be directed to the food court or institution support team.

8. Policy updates

This framework may be updated when the service, integrations or applicable requirements change. The published version should include an effective date and an appropriate support contact before launch.
